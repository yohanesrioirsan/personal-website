## About

FaceitOBS is a web application that lets streamers showcase their FACEIT stats directly on their stream using a clean and customizable overlay. ELO, K/D, win rate, and recent results update while you play.

It's a free, open-source side project built by players, for players.

## How it works

1. Enter your FACEIT name.
2. Pick a player card from the template library.
3. Copy the browser-source URL and drop it into OBS.

## Highlights

- **Template library.** More than a hundred player-card styles, from a simple vertical card to holographic, arcade, and terminal looks.
- **Test in frame.** Preview the overlay on top of real gameplay, then drag to reposition and resize it before it ever touches OBS.
- **No install.** The overlay is just a URL, so it works anywhere a browser source does.
