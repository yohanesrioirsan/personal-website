## About

invoilà is a simple invoice generator for freelancers, small businesses, and anyone who needs to send a professional invoice without a complicated setup.

Choose a template, fill in your details, and download your invoice. No account, no complexity, just invoices.

The interface takes inspiration from Path.cv, with a focus on typography, generous spacing, and a straightforward editing experience.

## How it works

1. Choose an invoice template.
2. Add your business details, client information, and invoice items.
3. Review and download your invoice.

## Highlights

- **No account needed.** Start creating an invoice without signing up.
- **Professional templates.** Present your work with a clean, readable invoice.
- **Straightforward workflow.** Go from a blank invoice to a downloadable document in a few steps.
- **Focused design.** A minimal interface keeps your attention on the invoice you're creating.
