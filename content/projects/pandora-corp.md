## About

Pandora Corp, one of East Indonesia’s biggest Esports organizations, wanted a website to showcase its achievements, events, and growing presence in the gaming world.

I led the entire development process from scratch, designing the structure, building with Next.js 15 + TailwindCSS, and collaborating with their graphic design team to bring the brand to life online.

End result? A digital arena that reflects Pandora Corp’s competitive spirit and future ambitions.
