## About

Boardsfx.fun turns your keyboard into a soundboard. Map sounds to keys and play them instantly, whether it's for streaming, calls, or just for fun.

## How it works

1. Download & Install the app
2. Import your sound effect
3. Set your key that you want to use
4. Fire the sound!
